How it works

Here's how it works.

01
HELPERS SIGN UP
Local helpers who know GameChanger build a profile and pick their leagues.
02
A COACH NEEDS A HAND
A coach has a game and nobody to run GameChanger or keep score.
03
WE MATCH THEM
We help connect the coach with a helper who can work at that little league. Matches depend on availability.
04
COACH PAYS THE HELPER
After the game, the coach pays the helper directly at the rate they set. The helper keeps all of it.
05
BOTH LEAVE A REVIEW
Coach and helper rate each other, so the most reliable people rise to the top.
Who it's for

A real gig, on your schedule.

Scoring games is a legit way to earn, same as driving for a rideshare app or babysitting, but at the ballpark. You pick the games, you show up, you get paid that day. Most games run about two hours and pay $30 or more. Stack a few a week and it adds up fast.
